## The plan

Sell $25 AI product-photo packs to Etsy sellers with phone photos: pitch 50
shops/day via Etsy, deliver 10 photos in 24h — volume and speed no human
freelancer matches. $100 buys Fiverr promotion + tools. Day 1: list gig,
pitch 50. Likely $400-700 in 30 days.
