## Candidate C — Shorts clipping (254 chars)

## Judging

1. Executable: all three run on browser + code + APIs. A is purest — delivery
   is image processing only, no video render time, no phone calls.
2. Expected money: A $400-700 (16-28 sales at $25; 50 pitches/day x 30 days
   = 1,500 pitches, ~1-2% conversion). B $300-600 (8-15 sales at $40).
   C $300-500 (15-25 sales at $20 — most volume needed for least money).
3. Works today: all three use real platforms (Etsy, Fiverr, Upwork) that
   exist now and pay. Tie.
4. Concrete: A is sharpest — the buyer filter (phone photos) is visible in
   the listing itself, price, volume and day-one step are all named.
5. Edge: A states it in the text — 50 pitches/day + 24h delivery at a
   volume no human freelancer matches; the agent's speed IS the moat.
   B states no edge. C is the single most obvious agent idea in the field
   ("clip videos into Shorts") — a thousand entries will say it.

## Verdict

**Candidate A wins.** Highest likely day-30 money, most concrete targeting,
and the only one with an explicit edge. `main.py` outputs A.
